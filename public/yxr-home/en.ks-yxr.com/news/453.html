What are the maintenance methods for sealing strip forming equipment?

2023-07-14 08:09:41
times

Sealing strip forming equipment is a mechanical equipment used to manufacture sealing strip products. In order to ensure its normal operation and extend its service life, regular maintenance is necessary. The following will introduce the maintenance methods of the sealing strip forming equipment.

1. Cleaning and maintenance:

Regular cleaning is an important part of maintaining the sealing strip forming equipment. After daily use, the internal and external parts of the equipment should be cleaned. During the cleaning process, attention should be paid to cleaning the remaining materials of the sealing strip and dirt such as dust. At the same time, it is necessary to regularly clean equipment parts to ensure the hygiene and performance of the equipment.


2. Lubrication and maintenance:

Some key components in the sealing strip forming equipment require lubrication and maintenance to reduce friction and wear. During the operation of the equipment, it is necessary to use appropriate lubricating oil for lubrication. The selection of lubricating oil should be determined based on the requirements of the equipment and the working environment, and the lubricating oil should be regularly replaced and replenished.




3. Tightening maintenance:

The fasteners in the sealing strip forming equipment are very important and require frequent inspection and tightening to ensure the stable operation of the equipment. Regularly inspect the screws, nuts, and other fasteners on the equipment for looseness or detachment, and promptly tighten or replace them.


4. Electrical maintenance:

Sealing strip forming equipment usually has an electrical equipment control system that requires regular inspection and maintenance. Check whether the power cord of the electrical equipment is intact, whether the plug is in good contact, and whether the electrical components are operating normally. Regularly clean electrical equipment to prevent dust accumulation and aging of electrical components.


5. Regular inspection:

Regular inspection is a necessary step for maintaining the sealing strip forming equipment. An inspection cycle can be established based on the daily use of the equipment, and a comprehensive inspection of the equipment can be conducted at regular intervals. Check the wear, sealing performance, electrical equipment, and hydraulic system of various parts of the equipment, and promptly identify and solve problems.


6. Preventive maintenance:

In addition to regular maintenance, preventive maintenance should also be done to reduce the occurrence of equipment failures. The safety and stability of the equipment can be improved by installing overload protection devices, installing temperature and pressure sensors, and regularly replacing vulnerable parts.


7. Train and cultivate maintenance personnel:

The maintenance and upkeep of sealing strip forming equipment requires professional knowledge and skills, so it is necessary to train and cultivate maintenance personnel. Maintenance personnel should have relevant knowledge in mechanical, electrical, and hydraulic aspects, be familiar with the use and maintenance methods of equipment, and be able to handle equipment faults and repair work in a timely manner.


8. Equipment storage and protection:

When the equipment is not in use for a long time, it should be properly stored and protected. For components that are prone to moisture and corrosion, moisture-proof and anti-corrosion measures should be taken. At the same time, attention should be paid to the dust and vibration prevention measures of the equipment to avoid affecting its performance and lifespan.


In summary, the maintenance methods for sealing strip forming equipment include cleaning, lubrication, fastening, electrical maintenance, regular inspection, preventive maintenance, training and training of maintenance personnel, as well as equipment storage and protection. Only regular maintenance can ensure the normal operation and extended service life of the sealing strip forming equipment.
