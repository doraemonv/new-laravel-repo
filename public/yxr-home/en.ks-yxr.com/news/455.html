What are the selection and usage techniques for sealing strip tooling equipment?

2023-07-14 08:14:40
times

Sealing strip tooling equipment is a commonly used tool for sealing and protection. While ensuring product sealing, it can also prevent the entry of dust, moisture, and other harmful substances, ensuring product quality and safety. Choosing and using appropriate sealing strip tooling equipment is crucial for improving production efficiency and product quality. This article will explore the selection and usage techniques of sealing strip tooling equipment.


Firstly, selecting the appropriate sealing strip tooling equipment requires considering the following aspects:

1. Material selection: Sealing strip tooling equipment is usually made of various materials, such as rubber, silicone, polyurethane, etc. When selecting materials, it is necessary to consider the product's usage environment and requirements. For example, if the product needs to be used in high-temperature environments, high-temperature resistant silicone sealing strips can be chosen. If the product needs to have good elasticity and wear resistance, rubber sealing strips can be chosen.




2. Size and shape: Select appropriate sealing strip tooling equipment based on the size and shape of the product. The size of the sealing strip should match the size of the product to ensure the sealing effect. If the shape of the product is complex, it can be considered to choose sealing strip tooling equipment with bending ability to better adapt to the shape of the product.


3. Sealing performance: The sealing performance of sealing strip tooling equipment is one of the important indicators for selection. The sealing strip tooling equipment should have good sealing performance and be able to effectively block the entry of dust, moisture, and other harmful substances. When selecting, you can refer to the relevant parameters and test reports provided by the manufacturer to understand their sealing performance.


Secondly, the correct use of sealing strip tooling equipment is also very important. Here are some usage tips:

1. Preparation before installation: Before installing the sealing strip tooling equipment, it is necessary to prepare the product and equipment. Firstly, ensure that the product surface is clean and free of grease, dust, and other dirt. Secondly, cut the sealing strip according to the size and shape of the product to ensure it matches the product.


2. Installation method: The installation method may vary depending on the sealing strip tooling equipment. Generally speaking, sealing strips can be installed onto the product using manual or mechanical pressure. During installation, it should be noted that the sealing strip should evenly cover the joints of the product to ensure the sealing effect.


3. Inspection and maintenance: After installing the sealing strip, it should be inspected to ensure its good sealing. If air leakage or poor sealing effect is found, replace or adjust the sealing strip in a timely manner. In addition, regularly clean and maintain the sealing strip, remove accumulated dust and dirt, and maintain its function and lifespan.


4. Monitoring and testing: To ensure the effectiveness and product quality of sealing strip tooling equipment, monitoring and testing can be carried out. For example, pressure testing instruments can be used to test the sealing performance of sealing strips to ensure that they meet the requirements. If problems are found, they can be adjusted and improved in a timely manner.


In summary, selecting appropriate sealing strip tooling and equipment and using them correctly are important factors to ensure product sealing and quality. When selecting, it is necessary to consider factors such as material, size, and shape, as well as sealing performance. When using, it is necessary to pay attention to skills such as preparation before installation, installation methods, inspection and maintenance. By selecting and using sealing strip tooling equipment correctly, production efficiency can be improved, ensuring product quality and safety.
