How to match the selection of sealing strip tooling equipment with sealant?

2023-07-17 11:04:21
times

The combination of sealing strip equipment and sealant is used to achieve better sealing effect and improve work efficiency. When selecting the combination of sealing strip tooling equipment and sealant, the following factors need to be considered: sealing strip material, sealant material, sealing method, and adhesive performance.


Firstly, the sealing strip material is one of the important factors in selecting sealing strip equipment and sealant. There are various types of sealing strip materials, including rubber, plastic, and silicone rubber. When selecting sealing strip materials, it is necessary to consider the characteristics and environmental conditions of the sealed object. For example, if the sealing strip tooling equipment is used for sealing operations in high-temperature environments, high-temperature resistant silicone rubber sealing strips can be selected. In a frozen environment, low-temperature resistant plastic sealing strips can be chosen. In addition, the hardness, aging resistance, and waterproof performance of the sealing strip are also factors that need to be considered when selecting the sealing strip material.


Secondly, the selection of sealant materials is also important. Sealant materials are usually divided into organic silicone, polyurethane adhesive, and polysulfide adhesive. Different sealant materials have different characteristics and properties, so the selection needs to be determined based on specific usage conditions and requirements. For example, for sealing environments that require high strength, high viscosity, and high temperature resistance, organic silicone can be chosen. For sealing environments with high requirements for chemical resistance, polyurethane adhesive can be chosen.




Once again, the sealing method is also an important factor in selecting sealing strip equipment and sealant. Common sealing methods include pre coating, adhesive strip, and potting. Pre coating involves applying sealant to the sealing strip and placing it in the position where it needs to be sealed. The adhesive strip type is to directly attach the sealing strip with adhesive performance to the position that needs to be sealed. The potting method is to pour sealant into the sealing position, and then solidify to form a seal. Choosing a suitable sealing method based on specific needs and working conditions can improve work efficiency and sealing quality.


The performance of sealant is also one of the key factors in selecting sealing strip equipment and sealant. The properties of sealant include adhesion, high temperature resistance, hardness, and chemical resistance. It is necessary to choose a suitable sealant based on the specific sealing requirements. For example, for sealed environments that require long-term immersion in water, a sealant with excellent waterproof performance can be chosen. For sealing environments that require high adhesion, a sealant with good adhesion performance can be selected.


In summary, the selection of sealing strip tooling equipment and sealant needs to consider factors such as sealing strip material, sealant material, sealing method, and glue performance. According to specific requirements and working conditions, selecting appropriate sealing strip equipment and sealant can improve sealing effect and work efficiency. At the same time, it is also necessary to pay attention to the quality and safety of sealing strip equipment and sealant, and choose reliable products and suppliers to ensure the smooth progress of sealing work.
